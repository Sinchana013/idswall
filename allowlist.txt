#    - "DNS Servers . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1"   <- Your DNS resolver
#    - "IPv4 Address . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.50"  <- Your laptop IP (already protected automatically)

# - Do not guess IP addresses. Use the real values discovered above.
